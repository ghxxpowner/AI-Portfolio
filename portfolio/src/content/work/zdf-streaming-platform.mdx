## About the project

At JAKALA, I played a key role in shaping the relaunch of the ZDF Mediathek into a modern streaming platform. Together with the UX team, I translated concepts into thoughtful interfaces for web, native apps, and smart TVs to ensure a consistent user experience. My focus was on designing an intuitive navigation structure and a visual language for automated, algorithmically driven page types. Acting as the bridge to development, I also guaranteed that all design specifications were technically feasible and met the highest accessibility standards.
